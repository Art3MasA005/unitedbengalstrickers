The Strikers Philosophy

A Concern of United Bengal Group

The United Bengal Strikers are a competitive cricket team representing a new generation of organized and professionally branded cricket in Bangladesh. The team traces its origins back to 2019, when it was initially established under the name Galaxy.

In 2026, the team entered a new chapter in its journey during the RHHS Premier League, undergoing a complete rebranding as the United Bengal Strikers. While retaining its original foundation, spirit, and connection among its members, the new identity introduced a more professional vision inspired by modern international franchise-level sports organizations.

United Bengal Strikers operates as a concern of United Bengal Group, aiming to develop the team beyond conventional local cricket into a full-scale sporting institution with structured management, distinctive branding, digital presence, and long-term youth development.

The Evolutionary Journey

Franchise Legacy

From a grassroots brotherhood in 2019 to premier tournament semi-finalists in 2026.

2019

The Foundation: Team Galaxy

Established in Rajshahi under the banner of Galaxy by a core group of passionate cricketers. Built upon years of friendship, unity, and an insatiable love for competitive cricket.

2021 – 2023

Brotherhood & Local Circuit Glory

Competing across high-intensity regional tournaments in Rajshahi, the team cemented its reputation for explosive powerplay batting, fierce pace bowling, and unmatched squad chemistry.

2024

United Bengal Group Concern

Transitioning from a conventional sports group into an organized sports division under United Bengal Group, establishing corporate governance, kit sponsorships, and structured management.

2026

RHHS Premier League Semi-Finalists

Undergoing a monumental rebranding as United Bengal Strikers with the battle cry “Born to Strike”. Entered the prestigious RHHS Premier League and stormed all the way to the Semi-Finals, marking the dawn of a franchise era.
